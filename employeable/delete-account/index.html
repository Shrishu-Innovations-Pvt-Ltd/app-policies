Privacy Policy for Employeable

Effective date: 13 October 2026 · Last updated: 13 October 2026

Employeable ("the app", "we", "us") is an AI-powered career analysis app for job seekers and people working in or entering IT. You upload your resume as a PDF, choose a target role, and the app gives you an employability score, skill gap analysis, a six-week learning roadmap and an AI chat assistant for roadmap tasks. You can optionally add a GitHub profile and a portfolio website so they can be included in the analysis. The app is developed and operated by Shrishu Innovations Private Limited ("the developer"), which decides why and how your personal data is processed (the "data fiduciary" under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and the choices and rights you have.

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to shrishuinnovations@gmail.com.

Summary. Your account, analyses, roadmaps, task progress and AI chat history are stored only on your device. We do not run user accounts or a database for them on a server. When you request an analysis, roadmap or AI chat reply, the information needed for that request (your resume contents, target role, optional GitHub and portfolio links, and your chat messages) is sent to our processing server, which passes the relevant parts to an AI model to produce the result. Your resume file is deleted after the analysis is finished and our server does not keep your data. We do not show ads, do not use advertising IDs, do not run analytics SDKs, do not access your contacts, and do not sell your data.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information you provide

  • Account details: your name, email address and password, used to create a local account on your device.
  • Resume: a PDF file you select. It contains whatever you put in it, which commonly includes your name, contact details, education, work history, skills and projects.
  • Target role: the job role you want to be assessed for.
  • Optional links: a GitHub username or profile URL and a portfolio website URL.
  • Chat messages: questions and replies you type into the AI assistant on a roadmap task.

1.2 Information created by the app

  • Analysis results: your employability score and its breakdown, skills found in your resume, missing skills, written feedback, and, if you provided links, public GitHub information (such as number of public repositories, main languages, whether READMEs exist, and last activity) and whether your portfolio website was reachable.
  • Roadmap and progress: your six-week roadmap, including suggested video links, and which tasks you have marked complete.
  • AI chat history: the conversation for each roadmap task.
  • Session information: an internal ID that keeps you signed in on your device.

1.3 Information we do not collect

  • No access to your contacts, calendar, SMS, camera, microphone or location.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising or marketing SDKs.
  • No analytics or behavioural-tracking SDKs.
  • No access to your files or photo library, other than the single PDF you choose through the system file picker.

2. Data stored only on your device

The following is stored in the app's local database and settings on your device and is not uploaded to us for storage:

  • Your account details. Your password is not stored as plain text: only a salted SHA-256 hash is kept.
  • Your analyses, scores, skills, feedback and, if provided, GitHub and portfolio results.
  • Your roadmaps, task progress and AI chat history.
  • A short text excerpt taken from the analysis feedback, kept to support your results screen.
  • The signed-in user ID in the app's preferences storage.

The system file picker may place a temporary copy of your PDF in the app's cache while you select it. The app does not keep your resume file as part of your saved data. The app's local database is not separately encrypted, so please protect your device with a screen lock. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes all of the above.

4. How your resume and requests are processed

The app does not analyse your resume on your device. When you request an analysis, roadmap or AI chat reply, the app sends the request over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our processing server, which is hosted on Render. The server then:

  • reads the text of your resume PDF and sends the relevant content, with your target role, to an AI model to produce your score, skills and feedback;
  • if you gave a GitHub username or URL, requests public information about that profile from the GitHub API;
  • if you gave a portfolio URL, makes a request to that website to check whether it is reachable;
  • sends your target role and missing skills to the AI model to build your roadmap, and uses the YouTube Data API to check that suggested videos exist;
  • sends your chat message, the task details and the earlier messages from that task conversation to the AI model to produce a reply.

Your resume file is deleted once the analysis is finished. Our server does not keep a database of your resumes, results, roadmaps or chats, and does not use accounts or user IDs. Our hosting provider may keep standard infrastructure logs, such as IP addresses and request times, as part of running the service.

Please do not include sensitive information you do not want processed (for example government ID numbers, bank details or health information) in your resume or chat messages. Anything in the resume text may be passed to the AI model.

6. App permissions

  • Network access: to send your requests to our processing server and receive results.
  • Selecting your resume: uses the Android system file picker, so no storage permission is needed.

The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts or notification permissions.

7. Consent and withdrawing consent

By creating an account, uploading your resume and using the app, you consent to the processing of your information as described in this policy. You can withdraw consent at any time by stopping use of the app, deleting your analyses or account in the app where available, or uninstalling it, which removes all locally stored data. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before you withdrew it. Because the app needs to send your resume for processing in order to work, it cannot analyse your resume without that processing.

9. Security and data breaches

Data is sent between the app and our processing server, and between the server and the service providers listed above, over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Your password is stored as a salted hash. Data kept in the app's local database on your device is not separately encrypted, so please protect your device with a screen lock. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

If a personal data breach affects you, we will notify you and the relevant authorities without undue delay, as required by applicable law, and tell you what happened, what data was involved and what you can do.

10. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we process about you and how it is processed.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data. You can also upload a new resume to get an updated analysis.
  • Erase your personal data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (Section 7).
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights on your behalf if you die or become unable to do so.

To use any of these rights, email shrishuinnovations@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity first. We aim to respond within 30 days.

Grievances and complaints

If you have a concern about how your data is handled, contact the grievance contact in Section 14. We will acknowledge your complaint and respond within a reasonable time, and in any case within 90 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

11. Children's privacy and who can use the app

Employeable is intended for adults aged 18 and over who are looking for work or working in or entering the IT field. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you are under 18, please do not use the app. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, email shrishuinnovations@gmail.com and we will delete it.

12. Information about other people

A resume often contains information about other people, such as references or former colleagues. Only include details about others that you are entitled to share. If you provide a GitHub profile or portfolio link, only public information is requested.

13. Where data is processed, and changes to this policy

Our service providers operate servers in several countries, so your information may be processed outside India, in countries with different data protection laws. Where required, we rely on the safeguards offered by those providers.

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If we make material changes, we will also notify you in the app before they take effect where appropriate, and ask for your consent again if the law requires it.
