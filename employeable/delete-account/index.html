Delete Your Employeable Account

App: Employeable · Developer: Shrishu Innovations Private Limited · Last updated: 13 October 2026

You can delete your Employeable account and the data associated with it at any time. This page explains how, what is deleted, and what happens afterwards.

Important. Employeable does not keep user accounts on a server. Your account, analyses, roadmaps, task progress and AI chat history are stored only on your device, so you can delete them yourself, immediately, without waiting for us.

How to delete your account and data

Option 1: Clear the app's data (keeps the app installed)

  1. Open your phone's Settings and go to Apps (or Apps & notifications).
  2. Select Employeable, then Storage.
  3. Tap Clear data (on some phones, Clear storage) and confirm.

Option 2: Uninstall the app

  1. Press and hold the Employeable icon, or find it in your phone's Settings under Apps.
  2. Tap Uninstall and confirm.

Option 3: Ask us by email

  1. Send an email to shrishuinnovations@gmail.com with the subject "Employeable account deletion request".
  2. Send it from the email address you used to create your Employeable account, so we can verify the request is yours. If you cannot, tell us that email address and we may ask for additional verification.
  3. We will check whether we hold any data about you and delete it. Because your account lives on your device, this will usually mean there is nothing stored on our side to remove, and we will tell you so. You will still need to use Option 1 or 2 to remove the data on your own device.

What is not covered

  • Your original resume file on your device (for example in Downloads or Google Drive) is not part of the app's data. Delete it yourself if you want it gone.
  • Your public GitHub profile and portfolio website belong to you and are not affected. Employeable only reads public information from them.
  • The AI model provider that processes your resume content and chat messages to produce results is a separate service and handles that data under its own retention practices. We do not control how long it keeps request data. See the privacy policy for the providers involved.
  • Our hosting provider may keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP address and request time) for security and operations under its own policies. These are not linked to an account, because we do not run accounts, and are not a retained copy of your resume or results.
  • We may keep information where the law requires us to.

Timing

Clearing the app's data or uninstalling removes your locally stored data immediately. For email requests, once your request is verified we complete any deletion on our side within 30 days. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone, and we cannot restore data that was only stored on your device.

Partial deletion

If you only want some data removed, for example a specific analysis or roadmap, without deleting your whole account, you can delete it inside the app where that option is available. If it is not available, or you want something removed from our side, email shrishuinnovations@gmail.com and tell us what to remove.
