Delete Your Tether Us Account

App: Tether Us · Developer: Shrishu Innovations Private Limited · Last updated: 5 October 2026

You can delete your Tether Us account and the data associated with it at any time. This page explains how, what is deleted, and what happens afterwards.

How to request deletion

Deletion requests are handled by email:

  1. Send an email to shrishuinnovations@gmail.com with the subject "Tether Us account deletion request".
  2. Send it from the email address you registered with, so we can verify the account belongs to you. If you cannot, tell us the registered email address and we may ask for additional verification.
  3. We will confirm once your account and data have been deleted.

What is not covered

  • Data on your own device (local drawings, cached nudges, and "Remember me" credentials, if enabled) is removed when you log out and uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your device settings.
  • Drawings you saved to your gallery stay in your gallery until you delete them yourself.
  • Content saved by your partner on their device is outside our control.
  • Live audio and video from calls is never stored, so there is nothing to delete.
  • Our service providers may keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP address) for security and operations under their own policies. These are not linked to a retained copy of your account content.

Timing

Once your request is verified, we delete your account and associated data from our systems within 30 days. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Effect on your partner

Chats and nudges are shared between two connected partners. When your account is deleted, content you sent may no longer be visible to your partner, and the connection between your accounts is removed.

Partial deletion

If you only want some data removed (for example, your live location, or specific voice messages) without deleting your whole account, email shrishuinnovations@gmail.com and tell us what to remove. You can also turn off location sharing at any time inside the app.
