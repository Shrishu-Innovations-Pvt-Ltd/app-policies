Privacy Policy for Tether Us

Effective date: 5 October 2026 · Last updated: 5 October 2026

Tether Us ("the app", "we", "us") is a private app for couples. It offers chat, voice messages, shared drawings ("nudges"), audio and video calls, screen sharing during calls, and optional location sharing between two connected partners. The app is developed and operated by Shrishu Innovations Private Limited ("the developer"), which decides why and how your personal data is processed (the "data fiduciary" under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and the "controller" under laws such as the GDPR). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and the choices and rights you have.

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to shrishuinnovations@gmail.com.

Summary. You create an account with an email and password. Your account details, chats, nudges, voice messages, call history and (if you turn it on) live location are stored on cloud servers so they can reach your partner. Calls and screen sharing are streamed live and are not recorded. We do not show ads, do not use advertising IDs, do not run analytics SDKs, do not use AI services, do not access your contacts, and do not sell your data. Tether Us is for adults aged 18 and over.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information you provide

  • Account details: your name, email address and password. You can also optionally enter your partner's email address at sign-up; you can skip this.
  • Content you create: text chat messages, voice messages, nudges (drawings made of strokes, shapes and text), and message reactions.
  • Partner connection data: the 6-digit pairing code you generate or enter, or the partner email address you use to connect. Pairing codes expire after ten minutes.

1.2 Information collected while you use the app

  • Online status: whether you are online or offline and your last-seen time, which your connected partner can see.
  • Chat activity: read and delivered status of messages, and typing indicators.
  • Call information: call history records (missed, ended and declined calls and their durations) and live call state used to ring and connect a call.
  • Push notification token: a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token that identifies your app installation so we can deliver notifications about nudges and incoming calls.
  • Device and connection data: our service providers (Section 4) receive technical data when your device connects to them, such as your IP address, device model, operating system version, app identifiers and network connection type. The app also checks whether your device is online so it can sync data. We do not use this data to track you across other apps or websites.

1.3 Information from device features you choose to use

  • Precise location (optional): only if you open the map and turn on location sharing, the app reads your device's GPS position (latitude, longitude and timestamp) while the app is in use and stores it so your partner can see it. The app does not use background location.
  • Microphone: to record voice messages and for audio and video calls.
  • Camera: for video calls only.
  • Screen content (optional): if you choose to share your screen during a call, whatever is shown on your screen, which can include private information such as notifications, is transmitted live to your partner for as long as sharing is on.
  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth): used only to route call audio to a connected headset or speaker. The app does not scan for, collect or store information about Bluetooth devices.

1.4 Live audio, video and screen sharing

During calls, your microphone, camera and (if you start it) screen streams are transmitted in real time through LiveKit Cloud to your partner. These streams are not recorded and not stored by the app or by us.

1.5 Information we do not collect

  • No access to your contacts, calendar or SMS.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising or marketing SDKs.
  • No analytics or behavioural-tracking SDKs.
  • No AI services receive your data.
  • No access to your photo or media library, other than saving a drawing to your gallery when you choose to.
  • No background location.

2. Data stored only on your device

The following stays on your device and is not uploaded to our servers, unless stated otherwise:

  • Your in-progress drawing and drawing elements (strokes, shapes and text), kept in a local database so the drawing canvas works offline.
  • A local cache of nudges you have sent and received.
  • Your partner's display name, held in memory only and cleared when you log out.
  • Voice notes you record are held temporarily in your device's temporary storage and are deleted after they have been uploaded.
  • "Remember me" credentials: if you enable "Remember me" on the login screen, your email address and password are saved on your device in the app's local preferences storage so you do not have to type them again. This storage is not encrypted, so the password is kept in plain text on your device. Anyone with access to your unlocked device, a rooted device, or a device backup could read it. If you share your device or are not comfortable with this, leave "Remember me" turned off. Turning it off and logging out removes the saved credentials.

5. App permissions

  • Microphone: voice messages and audio/video calls.
  • Camera: video calls only, requested when you start or answer a video call. The app does not require a camera to be installed.
  • Precise and approximate location: the partner location feature, requested only when you open the map and choose to share. Foreground use only.
  • Notifications: alerts for incoming nudges and calls.
  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth connect): routing call audio to headsets and speakers.
  • Screen capture and foreground service (media projection): only while you share your screen during a call. Android shows a system prompt before capture starts and a notification while it runs.
  • Network access and network state: to connect to our service providers and detect whether you are online.
  • Audio settings: to switch between speaker, earpiece and headset during calls.
  • Storage / photos (older Android versions only): used only when you choose to save a drawing to your gallery.

You can change or revoke any permission at any time in your device's Settings. Some features will not work without the related permission.

6. Consent and withdrawing consent

By creating an account and using the app, you consent to the processing of your information as described in this policy. Location sharing, microphone, camera, screen capture and notifications each require a further choice or permission from you, which you can withdraw at any time:

  • Turn location sharing off in the app, or revoke location permission in your device settings.
  • Revoke microphone, camera, notification or other permissions in your device settings.
  • Withdraw consent entirely by asking us to delete your account: email shrishuinnovations@gmail.com from the address you registered with, with the subject "Tether Us account deletion request".

Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before you withdrew it. If you withdraw consent that the app needs in order to work, we will delete your data as described in Section 7, unless the law requires us to keep it.

8. Security and data breaches

Data is transmitted between the app and our service providers over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS, and secure WebSocket for calls), and our cloud providers encrypt stored data at rest. Access to stored data is restricted by account authentication and database security rules. Data kept in the app's local database on your device is not separately encrypted, so please protect your device with a screen lock. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

If a personal data breach affects you, we will notify you and the relevant authorities without undue delay, as required by applicable law, and tell you what happened, what data was involved and what you can do.

To use any of these rights, email shrishuinnovations@gmail.com from the address you registered with. We may need to verify your identity first. We aim to respond within 30 days.

10. Children's privacy

Tether Us is intended only for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you are under 18, please do not create an account. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, email shrishuinnovations@gmail.com and we will delete it.

11. Information about other people

If you enter your partner's email address, or connect with a partner, you tell us that you are doing so with their knowledge. We use a partner's email address only to match and connect their account to yours. Content you send to your partner, including chats, voice messages, drawings, location and call or screen-sharing content, is visible to them, so only connect with someone you trust. If someone entered your email address and you do not want it stored, email us and we will remove it.

12. International data transfers

Our service providers operate servers in several countries, so your information may be processed outside the country where you live, including outside India, in countries with different data protection laws. Where required, we rely on the safeguards offered by those providers.
